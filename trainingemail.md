# FY27 Infra CSA Certification Requirements

**Date:** September 4, 2026, 1:49 PM

Hi Infra CSAs and Managers,

We are updating the FY27 certification requirements for Infra CSAs.

## Updated Requirements

Infra CSAs are required to complete:

- **GH-300:** GitHub Copilot
- **One of the following:**
  - **DP-300:** Azure Database Administrator Associate
  - **DP-800:** SQL AI Developer Associate

## Why Are We Making This Change?

Infra CSAs are expected to support and lead database migration and modernization engagements, including moving on-premises databases to Azure and modern database services.

DP-300/DP-800 provides the appropriate foundation for these scenarios and is a practical steppingstone towards more advanced database modernization capabilities. This change also recognizes the need to balance certification effort with customer delivery commitments, VBD accreditations, and available skilling capacity.

Managers should ensure GH-300 and the selected database certification (DP-300 or DP-800) are reflected in FY27 development and skilling plans.

## How to Select DP-300 vs. DP-800

Both certifications support Infra CSA success in database migration and modernization engagements. The right choice depends on your current level of database experience and your learning goals.

### Start With DP-300 If You

- Are new to database concepts or database administration.
- Want to build credibility in customer conversations about SQL Server, Azure SQL, migration, modernization, security, availability, and performance.
- Need a broad foundation in how databases are deployed, managed, and operated.
- Are looking for the recommended starting point for most Infra CSAs.

### Consider DP-800 If You

- Already have a solid understanding of database fundamentals and administration.
- Regularly work with database development, data platforms, or advanced modernization scenarios.
- Want to deepen your expertise in AI-enabled database solutions and application-focused database capabilities.
- Are looking to build on an existing database skillset rather than establishing one.

### Rule of Thumb

- "I'm building my database foundation and want to confidently engage in customer conversations." -> **DP-300**
- "I already have database fundamentals and want to advance into more specialized database solution development." -> **DP-800**

## FAQ

### 1. What Certifications Are Required for Infra CSAs in FY27?

GH-300 and either DP-300 or DP-800 are required.

### 2. Is DP-800 Still Required?

No. Infra CSAs must complete either DP-300 or DP-800, along with GH-300.

### 3. Which Certification Should Be Completed First?

Choose the certification that best aligns with your current experience and development goals. For most Infra CSAs who are building database knowledge, DP-300 will be the best starting point. If you already have a strong database foundation, DP-800 may be a better fit. GH-300 should be completed according to your agreed skilling plan.

### 4. What If I Have Already Started or Completed DP-800?

DP-800 fully satisfies the database certification requirement for FY27. If you have already completed DP-800, no additional database certification is required. Focus on completing GH-300.

### 5. What Should Managers Do?

Managers should review their CSAs' current certification status and ensure the two required certifications are reflected in FY27 development and skilling plans.

### 6. What Does the FY27 CAIP CSU Certification Picklist Mean?

All the certs will be available as part of recommended cert list in Viva Learn. GH-300 and DP-300 or DP-800 will be required certs in FY27.

### 7. When Will the DP-800 Requirement in Viva Learn Be Updated?

Infra CSA Viva Learn will update to reflect DP-300/DP-800 and GH-300 as required certs in the week of September 7.
